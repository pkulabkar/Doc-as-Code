# Monitoring and Troubleshooting Guide   
In this document you will learn how to monitor and troubleshoot for potential issues.

After you begin the system, ensure that you keep an eye out for the following signals.   
## Signals
- Real-Time Monitoring
- Observe chromatogram peaks
- Monitor pressure levels
- Check system alerts

## Observations
Here are some issues that you may witness.   

| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |   
|-------|----------------|----------|   
| No peaks| Injection failure| Check autosampler|
|High pressure|Blocked column|Replace column   
Noise in system| Contamination | Clean System|
Irregular peaks| Bubbles| Degas sytem|
